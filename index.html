About

Language expertise becoming language technology.

I come from a background in Translation and Linguistics and am currently building my career at the intersection of data, natural language processing and language technology.

My work is especially focused on multilingual language data, Korean and Brazilian Portuguese, corpus development, data analysis and NLP.

I am interested in building tools and datasets that connect linguistic knowledge with computational methods, particularly for under-resourced language pairs.

In some projects and repositories, I may also appear as LAC Cavalcanti.
