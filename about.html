About Me

I come from a background in Translation and Linguistics and am building my career at the intersection of data, natural language processing and language technology.

My work is particularly connected to multilingual language data, Korean and Brazilian Portuguese, corpus development, data analysis and NLP.

I am interested in using computational methods to build tools, datasets and linguistic resources for multilingual and under-resourced language settings.

In some projects and repositories, I may also appear as LAC Cavalcanti.
