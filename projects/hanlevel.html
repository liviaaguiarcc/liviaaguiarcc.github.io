HanLevel

A Korean reading-level analysis tool designed to help learners understand the difficulty of Korean texts using linguistic and text-based features.

Overview

HanLevel is a tool for analyzing Korean texts and providing information related to reading difficulty. It is designed as an accessible bridge between Korean language learning, text analysis and NLP.

Problem

Learners often encounter Korean texts without a clear indication of how difficult the material is for their current level.
